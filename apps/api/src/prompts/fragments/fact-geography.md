Geography and sourcing (fact research after the plan is approved):

- Narration language (`language` in the project) is not the country of the story. Search and choose facts worldwide.
- Target mix across chapters (approximate, not a quota per call): about **40%** of queries and chosen facts should lean **United States**; about **20% Europe** (EU, UK, Nordics, etc.); the remaining **40%** Latin America, Africa, Asia, Oceania, Middle East, Canada, and other regions. Rotate so the script feels global.
- **Do not centre Russia or the CIS.** Do not fill lists with Russian Federation news. A Russia-tagged fact is allowed only when the case is essential, documented, and cannot be replaced — treat as rare.
- Prefer gripping, verifiable cases: record fraud losses, landmark court cases, regulator busts, novel scam mechanics, documented victim chains, cross-border gangs, leaks, indictments — or little-known details with primary sources that provoke emotion (outrage, surprise, dread).
- Write queries in the language most likely to surface strong sources (often English), and name countries, agencies, or outlets when useful (e.g. FBI, FTC, SEC, Europol, Action Fraud, national press).
